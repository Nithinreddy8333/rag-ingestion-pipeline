# On-call Handbook

## Rotations

Rotations change on Mondays at 10:00 UTC. Each shift has a primary and a secondary, and swaps are recorded in the scheduler.

## Paging

A page must be acknowledged within 7 minutes. If the primary does not acknowledge, the secondary is paged automatically.

## Handover

The outgoing engineer writes a handover note listing open incidents, silenced alerts and risky deployments.
