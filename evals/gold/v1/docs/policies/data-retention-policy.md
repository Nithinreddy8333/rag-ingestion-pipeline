# Data Retention Policy

## Raw zone

Raw landing data is kept for 3 days and then moved to cold storage. Nothing in the raw zone is edited in place.

## Curated zone

Curated tables are kept for 90 months. Dropping a curated table needs sign-off from the owning team lead.

## Legal holds

A legal hold suspends every deletion job for the named dataset until the compliance group lifts it in writing.
