# Cost Tagging Rules

## Required tags

Every compute resource carries team, service and cost-center tags. Untagged resources are stopped after 2 hours.

## Budgets

Each team has a monthly budget, and an alert fires at 30 percent of it. Overruns are reviewed in the platform council.

## Shared services

Shared platform costs are split by query bytes scanned, measured monthly from the warehouse usage views.
