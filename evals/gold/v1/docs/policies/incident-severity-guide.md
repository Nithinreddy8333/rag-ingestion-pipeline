# Incident Severity Guide

## Severity one

Sev1 means customer-facing data is wrong or missing. The incident commander is paged at once and updates go out every 3 minutes.

## Severity two

Sev2 means an internal SLA is breached but no customer sees it. The owning team responds within 80 minutes during working hours.

## Postmortems

Every Sev1 and Sev2 gets a blameless postmortem within five working days, published in the reliability wiki.
